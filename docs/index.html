The problem

Same footage. Same story.
Three different jobs.

Nothing about the content changes. What changes is what each platform accepts — and those rules shift without warning. The day a platform moves its safe area, every clip you already cut is wrong again.

Scope

What this is —
and isn't.

Not an editor

Keep CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci, OpenCut — whatever you already use. We prepare correct inputs for all of them.

Not generation

Making video is already nearly free. We don't make video. We decide how existing video should be shaped for where it's going.

Not a replacement

When the edit is genuinely creative, you should still make it. This handles the part that is mechanical.

Generation collapsed. Publishing didn't. The bottleneck moved from making video to deciding what to do with it — and that decision layer has not moved at all.

How it works

Three steps.
No new editor to learn.

  1. 01

    One video in

    Long-form, raw footage, or a finished cut. Whatever you actually have.

  2. 02

    You choose the platforms

    Not all of them. Only the ones you post to. Nothing is guessed.

  3. 03

    Correct cuts out

    Framed, paced, captioned and trimmed per platform. Drop them into whatever editor you use.
